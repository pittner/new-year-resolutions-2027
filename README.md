# New Year's Resolutions 2027

36 New Year's resolution ideas in 6 groups (health and body, mind and habits, money, relationships, work and learning, home, travel and joy). Each resolution has why it matters and a first step for this week, plus 12 questions to look back at 2026 and a 45-minute plan.

**Live page:** https://pittner.github.io/new-year-resolutions-2027/

- Or type your own resolution and first step
