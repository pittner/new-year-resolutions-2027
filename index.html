12 questions to look back at 2026 before you choose

  1. What am I most proud of from 2026?
  2. What drained my energy this year, and what gave it back?
  3. Which resolution from last year did I keep, and why that one?
  4. Which one did I drop, and what got in the way?
  5. What do I want to stop doing in 2027?
  6. Who do I want to spend more time with?
  7. What would make my body feel better a year from now?
  8. Where did my money go this year, and was it worth it?
  9. What did I learn this year that I did not know a year ago?
  10. What is one thing I keep postponing?
  11. What would a good ordinary weekday look like in 2027?
  12. If 2027 goes really well, what is different a year from now?

Questions

What are good New Year's resolutions for 2027?

Good resolutions are small, specific and come with a first step: walk 8,000 steps a day, save 10% of every paycheck, read 12 books, call your parents every week. This page has 36 resolutions in 6 groups, each with a first step for this week.

Why do most New Year's resolutions fail?

They are too vague, there are too many of them, and you stop seeing them after the first week. Pick three, write one first step for each and keep them where you look every day, such as your phone lock screen.

How many resolutions should I make?

Three is enough: one for your health, one for money or work, one for relationships or joy. Fewer goals get more attention.

How do I keep my resolutions?

Make them concrete (a number and a date), start with a step you can do this week, and keep them visible. A lock screen with your resolution reminds you every time you pick up your phone.
